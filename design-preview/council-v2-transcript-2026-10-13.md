# LLM Council, round 2: "Long White Cloud" layout (2026-10-13)

**Brief:** design only. Build the most visually attractive and interactive NZ-specific page possible, aimed at capturing as many leads as possible.

**Proposal reviewed:** 16 sections:
- cloud-break hero
- glow-worm trust band
- city quiz
- koru reasons
- ₹ cost calculator
- sunrise intakes
- map
- 7-step flight
- passport stamps
- documents checklist
- loans
- postcards
- FAQ
- aurora final form
- footer
- an exit pop-up

## Advisors (summarised)
- **Contrarian:** 14 lead captures but no lead path. Use one shared lead record and a pinned "My NZ Plan". Make the hero's first tap a quiz question. Allow 3 set pieces at most. Use one flight, not two. Merge cost and loans into "Can we afford it?".
- **First Principles:** visitors from paid ads give the page about 8 seconds. Put the quiz directly under the hero and merge the calculator into the quiz result. Keep the map. Use under 7 sections and one form.
- **Expansionist:** a live boarding pass is the spine and gets stamped by every interaction. Merge the quiz into the map. Show NZ time next to home time. Add "Share with Amma/Papa" and a "Future you" return-on-investment line. Make the glow-worms real students.
- **Outsider:** te reo words mean nothing without a gloss. Put rupees and loans high. Show a real face under the hero. Nine forms feel like a trap. Cut the stamps, the drag interactions and the checklist.
- **Executor:** build the cloud break, glow-worm canvas, aurora, quiz and donut calculator. Cut the koru node spiral, flying map cards, the second bird, slamming stamps, draggable postcards and exit intent. Use one modal component.

## Peer review
Letter mapping: A Executor, B Contrarian, C Outsider, D Expansionist, E First Principles.

**Strongest response:** B was picked twice, D twice and E once. D was also named the biggest blind spot by 3 reviewers, for its weight.

**Blind spots caught:**
- one unified illustration language and photo grade
- the koru is not the silver fern
- the Southern Cross is shared with Australia, so use Matariki instead
- manaakitanga (care for guests)
- DPDP consent
- message match
- reduced motion
- a +91 phone input
- contrast over clouds

## Chairman verdict (built)
**Concept:** "Long White Cloud: your boarding pass to Aotearoa". Paper-cut illustration with grain, Young Serif + Figtree, and one duotone photo grade.

**Colour arc:** dawn → glow-worm cave → fjord day → pōhutukawa dusk → Matariki night.

**Lead system:**
- one lead record and a pinned pass chip
- every widget says "Add to my plan"
- the phone number is asked for once, at the quiz result

**Sections:**
1. Cloud-break hero, where the pass builder asks for name, home city and subject (no phone yet)
2. Glow-worm cave: a student quote, stats, tappable glow-worms, loan partners
3. Quiz and map merged, with a phone-gated result
4. "Can we afford it?", with a donut chart, loan slider, "Future you" line and Share with Amma/Papa
5. Koru and 10 reasons that stamp the pass
6. Single-bird flight path with a documents checklist that stamps the pass, Feb/Jul intakes with countdown, postcards in a scroll-snap row
7. Matariki final, with the pre-filled boarding pass and FAQ
8. Footer with pōhutukawa

**Cut:**
- the exit pop-up
- stamps that slam in
- draggable postcards
- separate visa, checklist and loan sections
- the Southern Cross
- audio, haptics and live weather
- the PDF
