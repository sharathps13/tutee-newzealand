For parents

You're paying for this. Ask us anything.

Most of our calls include a parent. We explain costs, loans, safety and the return on the degree, in plain terms and in your language. You're welcome to visit our Chennai office before deciding anything.

The Tutee Connect team, Virugambakkam

Is my child safe there?

New Zealand is consistently ranked among the world's most peaceful countries. We arrange airport pickup and first accommodation.

Will they find Indian food and community?

Auckland, Wellington and Christchurch have large Indian communities, with vegetarian food and temples close to campus.

Can they work to cover some costs?

Most student visas allow part-time work. We confirm the current hour limit for their visa.

What happens after graduation?

Graduates can apply for a post-study work visa. We assess residence pathways on the free call.

Questions

Still deciding? That's what the call is for.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Talking to a counsellor costs nothing and there's no obligation. We call in the slot you pick.

Do I need IELTS?

Most providers ask for IELTS, TOEFL or PTE. We prepare you and book the test.

Can I get an education loan?

Yes. Our partners offer secured and unsecured loans covering tuition, flights and living costs.

Can my family come with me?

Partners and children apply for their own visas. Options grow once you hold a post-study work visa.

Is there a route to PR?

New Zealand has post-study work and residence pathways. We assess your profile on the call.
