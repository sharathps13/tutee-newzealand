Visa types

Four ways in. We'll pick the right one.

New Zealand has several student visa types. Here's how they differ. Your counsellor confirms the current rules with Immigration New Zealand for your case.

Fee-paying student visa

Study with a recognised provider after paying your full course fees. Part-time work is allowed. We'll confirm your current hour limit with Immigration NZ.

Most students start here

Pathway student visa

Study up to 3 courses, one after another, over up to 5 years on a single visa. Part-time work allowed.

For multi-step study plans

Exchange student visa

Up to 4 years in a recognised exchange programme. You apply from outside New Zealand.

For exchange programmes

Foreign government-supported student visa

Full-time study funded by a foreign government loan or scholarship, for up to 4 years.

For government-funded students
